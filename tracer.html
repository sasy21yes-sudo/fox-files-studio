How

Pen: click = corner anchor · click-drag = smooth anchor · click the first anchor = close · click on the path = add anchor · click an anchor = delete it · Alt+click / Alt+drag an anchor = convert corner ↔ smooth.
Direct: drag anchors and handles (handles stay aligned, Alt breaks them) · Shift+click = add to selection · arrows = nudge (Shift ×10) · Delete = remove.
Move: drag the whole shape.
Double click (any tool): on a segment = straight ↔ curved · on an anchor = corner ↔ smooth.
Space+drag or middle button: pan · wheel: zoom · 1–9: shape · Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z: undo / redo.
